About this map. This is an internal Tydel customer-discovery map for Swedish energy companies and grid operators in Uppsala, Mälardalen and nearby regions. It is intended to help the team identify organisations that may experience recurring pain around Ediel, meter-data exchange, market-message validation and specialist-heavy troubleshooting.

Priority 1 marks the strongest first-wave discovery candidates, balancing likely Tydel relevance with geographic and organisational accessibility. Priority 2 broadens the comparison set across regional operators. Expansion contains larger or strategically useful organisations better suited to later validation or pilot discussions.
Markers represent prospect locations for research and outreach planning. This is not an official electricity-network coverage map and should not be used for operational or navigation purposes.
